Required Reading Before You Film

SEVEN RULES FOR CLEAN FOOTAGE.

The analyzer is opinionated — small camera-angle mistakes shave 10+ accuracy points off your report. Treat these like a checklist, not suggestions.

RULE 01
Well-lit space
Plain background where possible.
RULE 02
Propped phone
Use a tripod or a stable surface — never hand-held.
RULE 03
Fitted clothing
Joints must be visible. Avoid baggy shorts or tops.
RULE 04
Exact camera angle
The angle listed for each exercise is the angle we score.
RULE 05
Slow, controlled reps
Three good reps beat ten messy ones.
RULE 06
Both sides, separately
For unilateral exercises, film left and right separately.
RULE 07
One take per clip
Trim before uploading; the analyzer expects a single attempt.
